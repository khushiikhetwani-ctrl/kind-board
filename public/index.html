What is this?

A notice board for strangers who want to talk.

Write a noteAnything on your mind. No word limit. Nobody knows it's you.
Someone writes backOther people read it and reply, gently.
A new page every dayEach day gets a fresh board. Old days stay readable forever.

Why it exists: sometimes you want to talk to someone and nobody is around. Here, somebody usually is.

A few kind rules

Be the stranger you'd hope to meet.

  1. 1
    Be kind. No insults, put-downs or hate. Ever.
  2. 2
    Never tell anyone to hurt themselves. If someone is struggling, be gentle.
  3. 3
    Stay anonymous. Don't share names, numbers or addresses, yours or anyone's.
  4. 4
    No spam or ads. This is a place for people, not selling.

Every note is checked before it goes up. Anything unkind doesn't get posted, and anything unclear waits for a quick approval.

      Keeping it kind

      1. Lead with kindness. Many people come here because they're lonely or having a hard day.
      2. No hate, harassment or put-downs. Slurs, threats and belittling words are blocked automatically.
      3. Never tell someone to hurt themselves. Ever.
      4. Everything is checked automatically. Unkind posts aren't published. Links and contact details wait for a quick approval.
      5. Seriously hurtful posts count as strikes. Two strikes pause an identity: it can still read, but not post.
      6. Stay anonymous. Don't share personal details, yours or anyone else's.

      If you're struggling right now, you deserve real support. In the US call or text 988. In the UK & Ireland call Samaritans on 116 123. Elsewhere, findahelpline.com lists free, confidential lines.
